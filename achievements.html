Quick answers

How many trophies has Cristiano Ronaldo won?

Ronaldo has won 35 team trophies as of 9 September 2026: 32 at club level with Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al Nassr, and 3 with Portugal.

How many Champions Leagues has Ronaldo won?

He has won the Champions League five times: once with Manchester United in 2007/08 and four times with Real Madrid, in 2013/14, 2015/16, 2016/17 and 2017/18. He was the competition's all time top scorer with 140 goals along the way.

How many Ballon d'Or awards does Ronaldo have?

Ronaldo has won the Ballon d'Or five times, in 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017, the second most in the award's history.

How many league titles has Ronaldo won?

He has won 8 league titles in four countries: 3 Premier Leagues, 2 La Liga titles, 2 Serie A titles and the 2025/26 Saudi Pro League. The season by season goal record behind them is on the goals by season page.

What has Ronaldo won with Portugal?

Three trophies: Euro 2016 and the UEFA Nations League in 2018/19 and 2024/25. Those nights are told in full on the through the years page.

Has Ronaldo won the World Cup?

No. Ronaldo has never won the World Cup. His major international honours are Euro 2016 and two Nations League titles, though he did become the first man to score at six different World Cups in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Which club has Ronaldo won the most trophies with?

Real Madrid, with 15 trophies in nine seasons: 4 Champions Leagues, 3 FIFA Club World Cups, 2 La Liga titles, 2 Copas del Rey, 2 Supercopas de Espana and 2 UEFA Super Cups.

How many individual awards has Ronaldo won?

More than 50, including 5 Ballon d'Or awards, 2 The Best FIFA Men's Player awards, 4 European Golden Shoes, 3 UEFA Men's Player of the Year awards and 15 selections in the FIFA FIFPro World 11.

How many times was Ronaldo the Champions League top scorer?

Seven times: in 2007/08 with Manchester United, then six seasons in a row with Real Madrid from 2012/13 to 2017/18.

How many trophies did Ronaldo win at Manchester United?

Nine across his two spells: 3 Premier Leagues, the 2007/08 Champions League, the 2008 FIFA Club World Cup, 2 League Cups, the 2003/04 FA Cup and the 2007 Community Shield.

How many trophies has Ronaldo won at Al Nassr?

Two: the Arab Club Champions Cup in 2023 and the Saudi Pro League title in 2025/26, which made Saudi Arabia the fourth country in which he has won a league title.

Did Ronaldo win anything at Sporting CP?

Yes, one trophy. He won the Supertaca Candido de Oliveira with Sporting CP in 2002, in his only season in the first team before joining Manchester United.

How many European Golden Shoes has Ronaldo won?

Four, in 2007/08, 2010/11, 2013/14 and 2014/15, awarded to the top scorer across Europe's leagues.

Where do these trophy counts come from?

They are an own compiled tally, checked competition by competition against club and federation records. The method is set out at the bottom of this page.

How these trophy counts are compiled

The total of 35 counts senior team trophies won as a member of the winning squad for Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al Nassr and Portugal. Individual honours such as the Ballon d'Or, the European Golden Shoe and FIFA FIFPro World 11 selections are counted separately and are not part of the 35, which is why some published totals differ.

Each competition and year is checked against club and federation records. The most recent addition was the 2025/26 Saudi Pro League, won in May 2026. Last updated 9 September 2026.
