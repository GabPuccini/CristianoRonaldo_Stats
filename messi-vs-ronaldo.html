Questions

Who has more goals, Messi or Ronaldo?

Cristiano Ronaldo, with 981 goals in 1,340 games. Lionel Messi has 936 goals in 1,182 games.

Who has the better goals per game record?

Messi, with 0.79 goals per game. Ronaldo averages 0.73. Ronaldo played mainly as a winger in his first seven senior seasons, one with Sporting CP and six with Manchester United in the Premier League, which was first in UEFA's league rankings in 2008 and 2009. He scored 123 goals in 323 club games in those seasons. He played as a forward from his move to Real Madrid in 2009 and scored 450 goals in 438 games for them.

Who has more assists?

Messi, with 409 assists. Ronaldo has 291.

Who has more Ballon d'Or awards?

Messi has won 8 and Ronaldo 5. Ronaldo has been shortlisted 18 times and Messi 17.

Who has won more trophies?

Messi, with 46 senior team trophies. Ronaldo has 35.

Who has more Champions League titles and goals?

Ronaldo, with 5 UEFA Champions League titles and 140 goals. Messi has 4 titles and 129 goals. Messi was injured and did not play in the 2006 final, which Barcelona won against Arsenal, but he was counted as a winner.

Who has more assists in the Champions League?

Ronaldo, with 42 UEFA Champions League assists. Messi has 40.

Who has more international goals?

Ronaldo, with 146 goals in 234 caps for Portugal. Messi scored 126 goals in 208 caps for Argentina before retiring from international football on 6 October 2026.

Has Ronaldo won the World Cup?

No. Messi won the 2022 FIFA World Cup with Argentina, and lost the 2014 final to Germany and the 2026 final to Spain.

How many penalties did Argentina get at the 2022 World Cup?

Five, all taken by Messi. He scored against Saudi Arabia, the Netherlands, Croatia and France, and Wojciech Szczesny saved his penalty against Poland. Four of his seven goals at the 2022 FIFA World Cup were penalties, which is 57.1 percent.

What is the head to head record between Messi and Ronaldo?

They have met 36 times. Messi has won 16, Ronaldo 11, and 9 ended level. Messi scored 22 goals in those games and Ronaldo 21.

Who scored more goals in El Clasico?

Messi, with 26 goals in 45 matches against Real Madrid. Ronaldo scored 18 in 30 matches against Barcelona.

Who has more hat tricks?

Ronaldo, with 66. Messi has 62.

Who has more free kick goals?

Messi, with 76 direct free kick goals. Ronaldo has 66.

Who has more penalty goals?

Ronaldo, with 184. Messi has 115. Ronaldo has missed 36 and Messi 35, so Ronaldo has scored 83.6 percent of his penalties and Messi 76.7 percent. Shootouts are not counted.

Who has more headed goals?

Ronaldo, with 158. Messi has 31.

Who scored more in a single season and a single year?

Messi, with 73 goals in 2011/12 and 91 in 2012. Ronaldo's best are 61 in 2014/15 and 69 in 2013.

Who is better, Messi or Ronaldo?

There is no official measure. Ronaldo leads in goals, international goals, UEFA Champions League titles and hat tricks. Messi leads in goals per game, assists, Ballon d'Or awards, team trophies and FIFA World Cup titles. Ronaldo has won titles with every club he has played for, in five countries: Portugal, England, Spain, Italy and Saudi Arabia.

Finalissima

The CONMEBOL UEFA Cup of Champions was played in 1985 and 1993. UEFA and CONMEBOL revived it in 2022 as the Finalissima, and Argentina beat Italy 3 to 0 at Wembley. It is one of Messi's four senior trophies with Argentina. The 2026 edition against Spain was cancelled; CONMEBOL declared Argentina the winners, UEFA did not recognise the result, and it is not counted here.

Negreira case

Between 2001 and 2018 Barcelona paid about €7.3m to companies owned by Jose Maria Enriquez Negreira, then vice president of the Spanish referees' committee, and his son. A Barcelona court is investigating the club for sporting corruption, with a trial expected in 2027, and UEFA is reviewing the case. Barcelona deny wrongdoing and no ruling has been made. Messi played for Barcelona from 2004 to 2021.
