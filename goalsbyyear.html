What was Ronaldo's best year?

See those goals by competition

Cristiano Ronaldo's best calendar year was 2013, when he scored 69 goals for Real Madrid and Portugal. His five highest scoring years all came between 2011 and 2015, at Real Madrid: 69 in 2013, 63 in 2012, 61 in 2014, 60 in 2011 and 57 in 2015. He has scored 40 goals or more in fourteen separate years and has never gone a calendar year without scoring since 2002.

How these numbers are compiled

Goals are counted by calendar year rather than by season, so a figure covers 1 January to 31 December and mixes two club campaigns with any Portugal matches played in between. Only official senior first team matches count, in all league, domestic cup, continental and international competitions, and youth and reserve football is excluded.

Every year is an own compiled tally, counted goal by goal from full match records and checked against club and competition sources. The 2026 total is updated after every match Ronaldo features in. Last updated 26 July 2026.
