Cristiano Ronaldo career statistics

What was Ronaldo's best season?

The same goals by calendar year

His best season was 2014/15 at Real Madrid: 61 goals in 54 games, 48 of them in La Liga, which is still the club's record for a league season. Six of his seven highest totals came in Madrid: 61 in 2014/15, 60 in 2011/12, 55 in 2012/13, 53 in 2010/11 and 51 in both 2013/14 and 2015/16. The seventh is the one people overlook: 50 for Al Nassr in 2023/24, in his late thirties. His best in England was 42 for Manchester United in 2007/08, the season that won him his first Ballon d'Or.

Ballon d'Or

Cristiano Ronaldo has won the Ballon d'Or five times and been runner up six times. He was shortlisted at all eighteen editions held from 2004 to 2022, the most nominations of any player in the award's history, with Lionel Messi next on 17.

What the nominations show

A Ballon d'Or is settled by one vote about one year, and one year can turn on a great deal besides football: a strong summer tournament, the story a season told, which league a voter happens to watch, how hard a club campaigns for its man. Reasonable people still argue about several winners. The shortlist is a different test. It is drawn up before a single vote is cast, and a player earns his place on it only by having been, across the whole year, among the best footballers in the world. Ronaldo passed that test eighteen editions in a row, at three clubs in three countries, first as a teenage winger at Manchester United and last as a centre forward in his late thirties. Wins record who caught the voters in a particular year. A run of nominations that long records who was never out of the argument, and it is that consistency, more than any single trophy, that makes the case for him as the greatest of all time.

The full career timeline

Every trophy won along the way

  1. Sporting CP 2002 to 2003

    1. August 2002
      The boy from Madeira

      He was 17 when Sporting gave him his first team debut, against Inter in a Champions League qualifier, and his first senior goals came that October, two of them against Moreirense. One season was enough. Manchester United played Sporting in a friendly the following August and signed him within days.

  2. Manchester United 2003 to 2009

    1. 16 August 2003
      The number 7 at United

      United paid £12.24m and Sir Alex Ferguson gave a teenager the number 7, the shirt of Best, Cantona and Beckham. Four days after signing he came off the bench against Bolton, and half an hour was enough to show Old Trafford what it had bought.

    2. 22 May 2004
      The first trophy

      A 44th minute header opened the scoring against Millwall in Cardiff, United won 3 to 0, and the FA Cup was his first medal in England.

    3. 12 June 2004
      First for Portugal

      His first international goal came at 19, a late header against Greece in the opening match of Euro 2004. Portugal lost that game and, three weeks later, the final to the same opponents in Lisbon. He left the pitch in tears, and spent twelve years putting it right.

    4. 21 May 2008
      Champions League in Moscow

      He headed United in front against Chelsea in the Luzhniki, missed his kick in the shoot out, and still ended the night with his first Champions League. It capped a 42 goal season, the middle one of three straight Premier League titles.

    5. 2 December 2008
      The first Ballon d'Or

      He won his first Ballon d'Or at 23, named on all 96 ballots. Five years earlier he had come to United as a winger known more for his tricks than his goals, and the award measured how completely that had changed.

  3. Real Madrid 2009 to 2018

    1. 6 July 2009
      A world record move to Madrid

      Real Madrid paid a then world record €94m (£80m), and around 80,000 people filled the Bernabeu just to watch him presented. Over the next nine seasons he scored more than a goal a game.

    2. 2 May 2012
      The 100 point title

      Madrid won the 2011/12 title with 100 points and 121 goals, both La Liga records at the time, and 46 of those goals were his. It was the first of only two league titles in his nine seasons in Spain, won against Guardiola's Barcelona.

    3. 24 May 2014
      La Decima

      His penalty in the last minute of extra time made it 4 to 1 against Atletico in Lisbon and sealed Madrid's tenth European Cup, the one the club had waited twelve years for. His 17 goals in that campaign are still the record for a single Champions League season.

    4. 10 July 2016
      Champion of Europe with Portugal

      A challenge from Payet ended his final after 25 minutes. He spent extra time on the touchline, limping and shouting instructions, until Eder's shot beat Lloris and Portugal had their first major title. Twelve years after the final in Lisbon, this one was won.

    5. 7 December 2017
      Five Ballon d'Ors

      The awards for 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017 joined the first from 2008. Only Messi has won more, and nobody else has more than three.

    6. 3 April 2018
      The bicycle kick in Turin

      His overhead kick for Madrid in a Champions League quarter final was good enough that the Juventus crowd stood to applaud it. Three months later Juventus signed him.

    7. 26 May 2018
      Three in a row

      Milan in 2016, won with his penalty in the shoot out. Cardiff in 2017, with two goals in the final. Kyiv in 2018. No club had won three in a row in the Champions League era, and it took him to five European Cups, with a hat trick in the 2016 Club World Cup final along the way.

  4. Juventus 2018 to 2021

    1. 10 July 2018
      The move to Juventus

      Juventus paid €100m, rising to €117m with add ons, which made him Serie A's record signing at 33. He won the league in each of his first two seasons and finished as the division's top scorer in 2020/21.

    2. Summer 2021
      The Euros record

      Five goals at Euro 2020 won him the Golden Boot and took him to 14 at European Championships, the record for the tournament. He has scored at five of them.

  5. Return to Manchester 2021 to 2022

    1. 1 September 2021
      Past Ali Daei

      Portugal were losing to Ireland in Faro with a minute left when he headed two goals, his 110th and 111th, to win the game and pass Ali Daei. No man has scored more for his country.

    2. 11 September 2021
      Home again

      United brought him back on deadline day in August 2021 and gave him the 7 again. Twelve years after he left, he scored twice against Newcastle on his second debut at Old Trafford.

  6. Al Nassr 2023 to now

    1. January 2023
      To Saudi Arabia

      Al Nassr announced him on 30 December 2022 and made him captain. Within six months Karim Benzema, the reigning Ballon d'Or winner, followed him to the Saudi Pro League, and a league most of Europe had never watched was on television everywhere.

    2. 5 September 2024
      Goal 900

      His 900th official goal was a first time finish from Nuno Mendes's cross against Croatia in Lisbon, in the Nations League. Nobody had reached the number before.

    3. 8 June 2025
      Nations League, again

      Spain led twice in the final in Munich. His equaliser took it to penalties, Portugal won the shoot out, and the Nations League was theirs for a second time. He was forty.

    4. 21 May 2026
      Champion in a fourth country

      Two goals against Damac on the last day sealed the Saudi Pro League, his first league title since Juventus. It made Saudi Arabia the fourth country in which he has won one, after England, Spain and Italy.

    5. 23 June 2026
      Six World Cups

      Two goals against Uzbekistan made him the first man to score at six World Cups, and Portugal's record scorer at World Cups. He had said before it began that this one would be his last.

    6. July 2026
      The road to 1,000

      He stands on 979 career goals, 21 short of a thousand official goals, a total no player has reached. The dashboard adds each one as it goes in.

Cristiano Ronaldo transfer history

The story behind each move

Clubs have paid a combined €247m for Cristiano Ronaldo across four transfers. The largest was the €117m Juventus paid Real Madrid in July 2018, but the one that shaped his career was the €94m Real Madrid paid Manchester United in 2009. It was a world record at the time, and it bought nine seasons in which he scored at better than a goal a game. He joined Al Nassr on a free transfer in January 2023.

How many goals has Cristiano Ronaldo scored?

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 979 career goals in 1,338 senior appearances for club and country, as of 25 September 2026. That is 0.73 a game across more than two decades, and no man has scored more in official football. The goals by year section shows how the total built up.

How many goals has Ronaldo scored this season?

Ronaldo has scored 3 goals for Al Nassr in 2026/27 so far, 3 of them in the Saudi Pro League. Across the 2026 calendar year, which takes in the World Cup, he has 22. Every campaign is set out in the goals by season section.

Which club has Ronaldo scored the most goals for?

Real Madrid: 450 goals in 438 games between 2009 and 2018, a rate of 1.03 a game. That is more than his totals for any two of his other clubs put together. The nine seasons are broken down in the goals by season section.

How many Ballon d'Or awards does Ronaldo have?

Ronaldo has won the Ballon d'Or five times, in 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017, and finished second in six more years. His eighteen nominations are the most of any player, and every edition is in the Ballon d'Or section.

How old is Cristiano Ronaldo?

Cristiano Ronaldo is 41 years old. He was born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, Madeira, and is still playing for Al Nassr and Portugal more than two decades after his debut. The through the years section tells the career in order.

What was Ronaldo's best season?

His best club season was 2014/15 at Real Madrid: 61 goals in 54 games in all competitions. Measured by calendar year rather than season, his best was 2013, with 69. Every season is compared in the goals by season section.

Frequently asked questions

How many assists does Cristiano Ronaldo have?

Cristiano Ronaldo has 291 career assists in 1,338 appearances, as of 25 September 2026, roughly one every 4.6 games. The total is counted here goal by goal rather than taken from a data feed, which is why it is higher than some published figures.

How many Champions League goals has Ronaldo scored?

Ronaldo has scored 140 Champions League goals, more than anyone in the competition's history: 105 for Real Madrid, 21 for Manchester United and 14 for Juventus. He finished as its top scorer in seven seasons.

How many international goals has Ronaldo scored?

Ronaldo has scored 146 goals in 234 senior caps for Portugal, the most by any man in international football. His 11 World Cup goals came across six tournaments.

How many free kick goals has Ronaldo scored?

Ronaldo has scored 66 direct free kick goals: 33 for Real Madrid, 15 for Manchester United, 11 for Portugal, 6 for Al Nassr and 1 for Juventus. The technique that made them famous, struck with the laces and almost no spin, was already there at Manchester United.

How many penalties has Ronaldo scored?

Ronaldo has scored 184 penalties, 18.8 percent of his 979 career goals. Real Madrid account for 81 and Al Nassr for 32. Take every penalty away and he still has 795 goals.

How many hat tricks has Ronaldo scored?

Ronaldo has scored 66 hat tricks, 44 of them for Real Madrid, which is nearly five a season over his nine years there. He also has 10 for Portugal, 6 for Al Nassr, 3 for Manchester United and 3 for Juventus.

Where do these Ronaldo statistics come from?

They are counted here by hand, match by match, from full goal and match records, then checked against club and competition sources. The method, and the choices behind it, are set out at the foot of this page.

When did Cristiano Ronaldo join Real Madrid?

On 6 July 2009, for a then world record fee of €94m. He stayed nine seasons and scored 450 goals in 438 games, more than one a game, all set out in the goals by season section.

How much did Ronaldo cost when he joined Juventus?

Juventus paid €100m, rising to €117m with add ons, on 10 July 2018. At 33 he became the most expensive signing in Serie A history, and he won the league in each of his first two seasons.

When did Ronaldo win his first Ballon d'Or?

On 2 December 2008, at 23, when he was named on all 96 ballots. He has won five in total, in 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017, and the Ballon d'Or section has every year he was nominated.

When did Ronaldo score his 900th goal?

On 5 September 2024, with a first time finish against Croatia in Lisbon in the Nations League. Nobody had scored 900 official goals before him.

Which clubs has Ronaldo played for and when?

Sporting CP from 2002 to 2003, Manchester United from 2003 to 2009 and again from 2021 to 2022, Real Madrid from 2009 to 2018, Juventus from 2018 to 2021 and Al Nassr since 2023, alongside Portugal since 2003.

When did Ronaldo become the top international scorer?

On 1 September 2021, when two late headers against Ireland in Faro, his 110th and 111th goals for Portugal, took him past Ali Daei. He has since taken the record to 146.

How many World Cups has Ronaldo played at?

Six, from 2006 to 2026. On 23 June 2026 two goals against Uzbekistan made him the first man to score at six different World Cups, at the tournament he had said would be his last.

Where is Cristiano Ronaldo now?

At 41 he is still playing for Al Nassr and Portugal. As of 25 September 2026 he has 979 career goals, 21 short of 1,000, and every total on this page is updated after each match he plays.

How many trophies has Cristiano Ronaldo won?

Ronaldo has won 35 team trophies as of 25 September 2026: 32 at club level with Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al Nassr, and 3 with Portugal. Every club he has played for won something with him.

How many Champions Leagues has Ronaldo won?

Five: once with Manchester United, in 2007/08, and four times with Real Madrid, in 2013/14, 2015/16, 2016/17 and 2017/18. He is also the competition's all time top scorer, with 140 goals.

How many league titles has Ronaldo won?

He has won 8 league titles in four countries: 3 Premier Leagues, 2 La Liga titles, 2 Serie A titles and the 2025/26 Saudi Pro League. The goals behind each one are in the goals by season section.

What has Ronaldo won with Portugal?

Three trophies: Euro 2016 and the UEFA Nations League in 2018/19 and 2024/25, the second of them at forty, with his goal in the final. Those nights are told in full in the through the years section.

Has Ronaldo won the World Cup?

No. Ronaldo has never won the World Cup. His major international honours are Euro 2016 and two Nations League titles, and in 2026 he became the first man to score at six different World Cups.

Which club has Ronaldo won the most trophies with?

Real Madrid, with 15 trophies in nine seasons: 4 Champions Leagues, 3 FIFA Club World Cups, 2 La Liga titles, 2 Copas del Rey, 2 Supercopas de Espana and 2 UEFA Super Cups.

How many individual awards has Ronaldo won?

More than 50, including 5 Ballon d'Or awards, 2 The Best FIFA Men's Player awards, 4 European Golden Shoes, 3 UEFA Men's Player of the Year awards and 15 selections in the FIFA FIFPro World 11.

How many times was Ronaldo the Champions League top scorer?

Seven times, more than any other player: in 2007/08 with Manchester United, then six seasons running with Real Madrid, from 2012/13 to 2017/18.

How many trophies did Ronaldo win at Manchester United?

Nine across his two spells: 3 Premier Leagues, the 2007/08 Champions League, the 2008 FIFA Club World Cup, 2 League Cups, the 2003/04 FA Cup and the 2007 Community Shield.

How many trophies has Ronaldo won at Al Nassr?

Two: the Arab Club Champions Cup in 2023, where he was top scorer, and the Saudi Pro League in 2025/26. The league title made Saudi Arabia the fourth country in which he has won one.

Did Ronaldo win anything at Sporting CP?

Yes, one trophy: the Supertaca Candido de Oliveira in 2002, in his only season in the Sporting first team before Manchester United signed him.

How many European Golden Shoes has Ronaldo won?

Four, in 2007/08, 2010/11, 2013/14 and 2014/15, each for finishing as the top league scorer in Europe. The 2013/14 award was shared with Luis Suarez.

How these numbers are compiled

Every figure on this site counts official senior first team matches for Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al Nassr and Portugal, in all league, domestic cup, continental and international competitions. Youth and reserve football is excluded, including Ronaldo's Sporting CP B and Portugal youth appearances.

Goals, assists, penalties, free kicks and hat tricks are counted by hand from full match records and then checked against club and competition sources. Assists especially: each one is credited goal by goal rather than taken from a data feed, which is why the total of 291 is higher than several football databases carry.

A few things are worth knowing about how the sections above divide that same tally. Calendar years run from 1 January to 31 December, so a year mixes two club campaigns with any Portugal matches played in between, while seasons follow the club calendar instead. The competition, finish type and body part splits each add up to 979, so no goal is counted twice or left out. Opponent records span whole careers rather than single spells, so a total such as the 25 against Atletico Madrid covers more than one of his clubs. The timeline is extended when Ronaldo reaches a new milestone rather than after every match, so its dates and its running total are checked together.

Figures are updated after every match Ronaldo features in. Last updated 25 September 2026.
