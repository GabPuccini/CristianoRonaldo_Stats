What was Ronaldo's best season?

The same goals by calendar year

Cristiano Ronaldo's best season was 2014/15 at Real Madrid, when he scored 61 goals in 54 games in all competitions, including a club record 48 in La Liga. Six of his seven highest scoring seasons came at Real Madrid: 61 in 2014/15, 60 in 2011/12, 55 in 2012/13, 53 in 2010/11 and 51 in both 2013/14 and 2015/16. His best outside Spain was 50 goals for Al Nassr in 2023/24, and his best in England was 42 for Manchester United in 2007/08.

How these numbers are compiled

Club seasons run from the first competitive match of a campaign to the last, so a season mixes league, domestic cup, league cup, continental and other competition matches. Portugal is listed by calendar year instead, because international football is played in windows across a season. Cells marked n/a are competitions the team did not play that season.

His two Sporting CP B appearances in 2002/03 are listed for completeness, but they are second team football, so they sit outside every total. The club career total reads 1,102 games, and the career figure across the rest of the site is 1,335: those same 1,102 senior club games plus 233 caps for Portugal. Goals are unaffected, since he did not score in either B team match.

Every row is an own compiled tally, counted match by match from full records and checked against club and competition sources, then updated after every match Ronaldo features in. Last updated 9 September 2026.
